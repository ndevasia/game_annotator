What to Annotate

As you play, consider noting down observations about:

  • Successes: Moments where you did something well or achieved a goal
  • Challenges: Moments where you struggled or made a mistake
  • Decision Points: Key choices you made and why you made them
  • Learning Moments: Times when you discovered something new about the game
  • Emotions: Notable feelings during important moments (excited, frustrated, confident, etc.)
  • Strategy: Your approach or tactics during different phases of the game
